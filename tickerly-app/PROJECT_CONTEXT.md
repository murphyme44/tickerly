# Tickerly

Tickerly is a mobile-first social stock market game where users and their friends become tradable stocks within private groups.

Users can:
- Create or join private groups
- Automatically become a stock/ticker in each group
- Post bullish or bearish events about other users
- Buy, sell, and short friend stocks using fake currency
- Comment on events
- Compete on leaderboards
- Track portfolio performance across customizable seasons

## Core Philosophy

Tickerly should feel like:
- fantasy football
- group chat humor
- social stock market chaos

The app prioritizes:
- humor
- social interaction
- competition
- viral friend-group moments

NOT realism.

## Market Mechanics

- Events act as catalysts
- Trading acts as an amplifier
- Events decay over time
- Users cannot post events about themselves
- Groups have separate economies/currencies
- Users are not anonymous
- Users cannot initially own their own stock
- Shorting is allowed

## Initial MVP Features
